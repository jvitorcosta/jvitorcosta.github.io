CarDeck, a taste of Pokémon nostalgia on my dashboard

Side project, 2026

What it is

A cheap ESP32 touchscreen on my dashboard that shows the song playing on Spotify as an old Game Boy-style Pokémon battle. A random Pokémon walks along the HP bar while the song plays, and synced lyrics appear in the dialogue box, including Japanese and Chinese text.

How it grew

A second screen became a companion display with an avatar that sings along. A phone page with a QR code works as a remote. Then I connected it to the car over Bluetooth OBD, so it also works as a gauge cluster with fuel economy, range and battery readings.

The part I'm careful about

Talking to a car's computer can go wrong. Every command CarDeck sends passes through an allow-list that only permits reading data, and tests prove that write, clear and control commands are refused.

Stack: C++17, Arduino, PlatformIO, ESP32 and ESP32-S3, Bluetooth OBD (ELM327), host-side unit tests, CI, tagged releases
