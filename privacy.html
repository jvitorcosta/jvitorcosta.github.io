Privacy

This page covers gws-cli, a small Google OAuth app I use only on my own computer, with my own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it accesses: files it creates itself in my Google Drive (backups of my own documents) and my own Google Sheets.
  • What it stores: its sign-in token stays on my computer, encrypted. Nothing is sent anywhere except Google's own APIs.
  • What it shares: nothing. No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

This website itself has no cookies, analytics or tracking.
